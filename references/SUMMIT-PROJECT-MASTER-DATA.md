## 02 — Beit Al Razzaz

- ID / slug: `beit-al-razzaz`
- Inbox: `project-inbox/heritage-restoration/beit-al-razzaz/`
- Content status: Source supplied
- Media status: `pendingMedia`

### Historical background

Bayt Al-Razzaz is a magnificent historical mansion located in the heart of Cairo's district, Darb Al Ahmar, the mansion was built on two stages.

Mamluk origins: the eastern part of the complex dates back to the 15th century, constructed during reign of the mamluk sultan Qaitbay. This section showcases the grandeur and intricate details characteristic of mamluk architecture.

Ottoman Expansion: in 18th century, the western part was added by Ahmed Katkhuda AL-Razzaz, a wealthy rice merchant. This expansion connected the two parts, creating a vast and impressive complex.

### Project overview

Summit construction was responsible for the structural repair, adaptive reuse and renovation of eastern part of Bayt Al Razzaz.

- Client: Ministry of Antiquities and Tourism
- Scope: Restoration and rehabilitation for the north eastern corner house
- Location: Al Darb Al Ahmar
- Project Duration: 10 months

## 03 — Safi Al Din Gowhar

- ID / slug: `safi-al-din-gowhar`
- Inbox: `project-inbox/heritage-restoration/safi-al-din-gowhar/`
- Content status: Source supplied
- Media status: `pendingMedia`

### Historical background

It was established by Safi al-Din Jawhar al-Nasiri al-Maliki, also known sometimes as Sidi Muqaddam of the Sultanate Mamluks during the days of the Bahri Mamluk Sultan Baybars al-Jashankir, in the year 714 AH/1314 AD. It is located on Rukibiya Street, branching from Sulaibiyah Street in the Khalifa neighborhood in the city of Cairo. It consists of an irregular rectangular space divided into a dirka (entrance hall) followed by the main entrance. This dirka leads to an open courtyard and the burial dome, which is preceded by a prayer area on its southeastern side.

### Project overview

Summit Construction Company was assigned as a company specializing in restoration works and everything related to restoration. The company was assigned to implement dewatering system to Safi al-Din Dome.

- Client: Megawra Office (Dr. Mai El Abrashi)
- Scope: Dewatering System
- Location: Al Saleeba Street
- Project Duration: 5 months
