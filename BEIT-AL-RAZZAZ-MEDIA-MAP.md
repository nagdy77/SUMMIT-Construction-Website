# Beit Al-Razzaz corrected media map

All supplied high-resolution photographs and raw media remain in their original folders. The website uses generated WebP derivatives only; no original source photograph or raw file was deleted, moved, overwritten, or modified.

## Confirmed project structure

### Phase 1 — Northern Side

Public labels:

- English: `Phase 1 – Northern Side`
- Arabic: `المرحلة الأولى – المنطقة الشمالية`

This phase contains the three verified comparison pairs supplied in the `E3-00-27-01` documentation and the 12 distinct Northern Side photographs, including the phase lead image. Its WebP derivatives are grouped under the public Phase 1 and Northern Side asset paths.

### Phase 2 — Western Wing

Public labels:

- English: `Phase 2 – Western Wing`
- Arabic: `المرحلة الثانية – الجناح الغربي`

This phase contains the verified `E3-01-37-05` comparison, the two explicitly grouped Western Wing comparison pairs, and 16 additional Western Wing photographs. The possible pairs in `E3-01-37-03`, `E3-01-37-07`, and `E3-01-37-09` remain regular Phase 2 gallery photographs because their chronology is not confirmed. Western set 3 still has one displayable JPG and two NEF files without a confirmed matching display image. Set 4 duplicates the verified `E3-01-37-05` pair and is not repeated.
